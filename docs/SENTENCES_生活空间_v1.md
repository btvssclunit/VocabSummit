## 六 · 熟食中心 `scene_hawker.png`

| # | 句子 | English | 目标词 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 我要打包一份鸡饭。 | I'd like a plate of chicken rice to take away. | 鸡饭 |
| 2 | 我想点一碗鱼汤。 | I'd like to order a bowl of fish soup. | 汤 · 鱼 |
| 3 | 这碗面很烫，小心！ | This bowl of noodles is very hot — be careful! | 面 |
| 4 | 我喜欢吃咖喱鸡。 | I like eating curry chicken. | 咖喱 · 鸡肉 |
| 5 | 这里有素食摊吗？ | Is there a vegetarian stall here? | ⚠️ 素食摊 |
| 6 | 我要一杯美禄冰。 | I'd like an iced Milo. | ⚠️ 美禄冰 |
| 7 | 我要一杯咖啡乌，少糖。 | I'd like a kopi-o — black coffee — with less sugar. | 咖啡 · 糖 |
| 8 | 这里的果汁比商场便宜。 | The juice here is cheaper than at the mall. | 果汁 |
| 9 | 新加坡人习惯用纸巾占位子。 | Singaporeans have a habit of using a packet of tissue to reserve a seat. | ⚠️ 纸巾 |
| 10 | 吃完后，我们要归还碗盘。 | After eating, we return our bowls and plates. | ⚠️ 碗盘 |

「注 #1」打包 (dǎ bāo) is the standard word for takeaway in Singapore and Malaysia — you'll hear "da bao" used in English conversation too. Elsewhere 外带 or 带走 is more usual.
「注 #2」You order and pay at each stall separately, not at one central till.
「注 #4」Curry chicken shows how food travels between Singapore's communities — Indian in origin, cooked across Chinese, Malay and Indian households, each with its own version.
「注 #5」Most hawker centres have a vegetarian stall for Buddhist, Hindu or personal reasons. Halal stalls are separately certified and keep their own crockery and washing area, so Muslim customers can eat safely at the same centre. Knowing how to ask this is a practical courtesy in a multi-religious society.
「注 #6」Milo is a malted chocolate drink most Singaporean children grow up on. Ask for a "Milo dinosaur" and you'll get it heaped with undissolved Milo powder.
「注 #7」Local coffee has its own ordering code borrowed from Hokkien. 乌 (o) means black, no milk. Condensed milk is the default unless you say otherwise, and 咖啡乌 already comes sweetened — which is why you must say 少糖 for less. Say 咖啡乌空 (kopi-o kosong) for none at all.
「注 #9」Known in Singlish as "choping". A tissue packet on a table means the seat is taken while you queue. It relies entirely on mutual trust and no one polices it.
「注 #10」⚠️ Returning used crockery to the tray-return station became mandatory at hawker centres in 2021. **Verify current rules before publishing** — enforcement details have shifted.

⚠️ **咖啡乌 为 tile-only**（owner 裁定）。已画好的 sprite 仍可作为 #7 的词卡插图。
⚠️ **未覆盖：** 米饭、饺子、茶。

「注 #9」The void deck — the open ground floor left empty under every block — hosts chess, weddings and funerals alike. Shared ground in the most literal sense.
